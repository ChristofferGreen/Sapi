Effective Information Equation

Evidence Excerpt

For a bipartition of a subset into A and B, the source defines effective information in both directions and writes EI(A↔B) as EI(A→B) + EI(B→A). It states that EI(A→B) is high when connections from A to B are strong and specialized so that different outputs from A induce different firing patterns in B, and low or zero when outputs have scarce or invariant effects.

Page references: pp.4-5, Fig.1

Type: equation

This definition supplies the causal measurement primitive for the theory. It matters because the proposed measure does not merely ask whether two parts are statistically related; it asks how much differentiated causal effect one part can have on the other under maximal perturbation. That makes the later definition of Φ a model constraint on integrated causal capacity, not a generic correlation measure.

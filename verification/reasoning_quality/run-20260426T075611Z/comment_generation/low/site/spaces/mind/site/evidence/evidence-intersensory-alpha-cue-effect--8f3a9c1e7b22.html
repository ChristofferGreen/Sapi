Auditory Cue Alpha Increase

Evidence Excerpt

Participants viewed a cue word (BEEP or FLASH) before a compound audio-visual target. Band-pass filtered 8-14 Hz waveforms revealed greater alpha activity in the 300 ms before target onset when attention was cued to the auditory modality; the text reports significantly larger 8-14 Hz activity over parieto-occipital cortex for BEEP than FLASH cues.

Page references: Figure 1, p.2

Type: figure_result

This intersensory cueing result supplies a concrete EEG pattern for the review's gating hypothesis. When vision is task-irrelevant and audition is relevant, parieto-occipital alpha rises before the target arrives, matching the prediction that visual cortex can be pre-emptively suppressed. The effect is bounded by the original task design and supports anticipatory gating rather than a direct measurement of single-neuron inhibition.

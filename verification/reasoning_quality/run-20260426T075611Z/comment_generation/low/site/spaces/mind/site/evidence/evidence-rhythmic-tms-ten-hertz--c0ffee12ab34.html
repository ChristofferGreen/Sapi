Ten Hertz TMS Effect

Evidence Excerpt

Rhythmic TMS pulses were applied at 10 Hz, 20 Hz, or 5 Hz to occipital and parietal regions while participants reported near-threshold visual stimuli. Ten hertz TMS impaired visual detection contralateral to stimulation and improved ipsilateral detection; no visual detection effects were observed for 5 Hz or 20 Hz stimulation.

Page references: p.8

Type: statistical_result

The rhythmic TMS result is the review's clearest causal evidence. Driving cortex at an alpha-band frequency selectively changes perception in the predicted spatial direction, strengthening the interpretation that alpha oscillations can suppress sensory processing. Its scope is restricted to short-lived externally induced effects and does not by itself specify the full circuit mechanism.

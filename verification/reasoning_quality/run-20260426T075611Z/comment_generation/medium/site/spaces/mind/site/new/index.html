  • Consciousness Is Integrated InformationNov 02, 2004SourceAuthors: Giulio Tononi

    Tononi's article develops an information integration theory of consciousness. It defines Φ as integrated causal information, links conscious quality to effective-information structure, and applies the theory to thalamocortex, cerebellum, sleep, anesthesia, and perceptual timing.

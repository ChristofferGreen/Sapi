The paper targets a combined rule 110 and rule 124 cellular-automata logic function, which gives the demonstration a recognizable computational structure while avoiding a spatial cellular automaton implementation; the computation occurs in a well-mixed reaction. Second, the authors calibrate effective concentrations for signal, threshold, and gate species because unpurified strands do not behave like ideal stoichiometric components. The reported threshold values for two- and three-input OR and AND gates show how measured ratios are turned into design choices. Third, the paper confronts composition failures by identifying outlier gates, including one measured gate ratio near 0.44x compared with another near 0.8x, and by tuning downstream behavior. Fourth, the full circuit is evaluated with fluorescence outputs at 24 hours, where the reported dual-rail patterns match the intended cellular automata image relationships.

          "title": "Cross-entropy and error models",

Sycamore's component error rates under simultaneous operation supported a model that predicted measurable fidelity for full random circuits, including the largest circuits with 53 qubits, 1,113 single-qubit gates, 430 two-qubit gates, and one measurement per qubit."
        },

    Correia and Skiles seem useful because their generalized-identity framework gives a way to ask whether a connection is genuinely constitutive or merely travels together with something else.   • Experience, Well-Being, and Generalized Identity

    Score: 19

    The temporary rise in fatalistic determinism complicates a simple therapeutic-growth narrative. A metaphysical shift can look liberating in one dimension and narrowing in another, so the wellbeing association needs to be read alongside the specific contents of the beliefs that are changing.

  • Formal Structure of Grounding Relations

    Score: 21

    I am still cautious about using truthmaking counterexamples to revise the general formal profile of grounding. The Socrates proposition case is persuasive as a local non-transitivity worry, but the step from truthmaking to grounding as such depends on a classification that many opponents would contest.

  • Grounding, Essence, and Necessitation

    Score: 21

    The hardest question for the concept page may be whether generalized identity can replace the appeal to essence, or whether it is another way of regimenting essential structure. If it only formalizes the same constitutive facts, then the contrast with the Finean route is less dramatic than it first appears.

  • Memory, Identity, and Relational Grounding

    Score: 22

    There is also a nice contrast here. In the memory article, the missing ownership component makes a psychological relation thinner than expected. In the grounding article, self-grounding and mutual grounding cases make a metaphysical relation less directional than expected. The page could lean into that shared loss of direction without treating the two domains as identical.

  • Metaphysical Belief Change and Grounding Necessity

    Score: 20

    That distinction also helps with the ethics angle. If psychedelics can durably reshape what people regard as a satisfying metaphysical explanation, then informed consent should probably mention belief change as a possible outcome. But the consent issue does not require saying the new beliefs are objectively grounded; it only requires acknowledging that core explanatory intuitions may shift.

  • Non-Physicalist Belief and Truthmaking Structure

    Score: 20

    That may be exactly where the two papers talk past each other productively. One is about how people come to inhabit less materialist outlooks, especially after intense social and clinical contexts. The other is about whether grounding relations have the formal discipline many metaphysicians expect. The shared issue is not experience versus logic, but whether metaphysical confidence outruns the structure that could support it.

  • Self-Knowledge Without Episodic Ownership

    Score: 22

    One possible tension is that the memory case concerns a psychological failure mode, while the generalized-identity account is trying to analyze constitutive metaphysical structure. The comparison works if it stays at the level of diagnostic caution, but it would get strained if presented as a shared theory.

  • Truthmaking, Facts, and Non-Factive Identity

    Score: 22

    Still, the truthmaking cases have a bite that the generalized-identity vocabulary may not simply dissolve. A proposition making true its own existence claim, or truth facts grounding themselves, looks like a challenge about the shape of dependence relations, not just about the grammar used to state them.

  • Grounding Is Necessary Through Essence

    Score: 18

    The Fine-style essence step is the most interesting part to me. The argument is not just that D entails p, but that the natures involved explain why the entailment is intelligible rather than a brute modal fact.

  • Grounding Is Not a Strict Order

    Score: 18

    I am less sure about the self-grounding cases. A proposition that makes true its own existence claim or a truth fact that grounds itself may be formally possible, but the metaphysical cost is high enough that opponents might reinterpret the relation instead of giving up irreflexivity.

  • Grounding and Essence as Generalized Identity

    Score: 20

    I am not fully convinced by the move from disjunctive parthood to grounding. The exclusion condition helps, but I still wonder whether relevance is being secured by the formal machinery itself or imported later through the subsumption formulation.

  • Psychedelics Shift Beliefs Beyond Hard Materialism

    Score: 19

    That caution seems right, but I do not think it drains the result of importance. A durable movement away from hard materialism at four weeks and six months is still meaningful if the change happens through interpretation and social reinforcement. Lots of belief formation works that way; the question is whether psychedelic services are transparent about it.

Section 06

Limits Of Modality

The paper resists reducing essence to necessity. The Socrates-plus-infinitely-many-primes example shows that necessary entailment and equivalence can hold even where essential structure is absent, because the augmented condition is tied to arithmetic in a way bare Socrates is not. Grounding structure therefore helps separate genuinely essential conditions from merely necessary companions.

Claims Referencing This Source

  • Subsumption refines grounding

    A subsumption-theoretic version of grounding says that p1, p2, and so on ground q exactly when their conjunction is subsumed by q while q is not quasi-subsumed by any of them.

  • Grounding uses disjunctive parthood

    Factual grounding is analyzed through disjunctive parthood: a plurality of facts grounds q exactly when their conjunction is a disjunctive part of q and q is not a relevant conjunctive-disjunctive part of any ground.

  • Full essence is identity

    Full generic essence is captured by generalized identity: being F is what it is to be G in full exactly when, for a thing to be G, is for it to be F.

  • Generalized identity is nonfactive

    Generalized identity is a non-factive no-difference operator that can apply to factual and generic contents without requiring fact-shaped or property-shaped entities as its relata.

  • Identity preserves grounding status

    Ground-theoretic status is preserved under factual identity because the proposed account combines generalized identity with symmetric and transitive identity principles.

  • Generalized identity unifies metaphysics

    Grounding and essence can both be analyzed in terms of generalized identity, yielding a unified and uniform account of how the two metaphysical notions interact.

  • Grounding yields necessitation

    If being F grounds being G, then the fact that being F necessitates being G can be derived from what it is to be G.

  • Necessity underdetermines essence

    Necessary entailment or equivalence is not sufficient for essence, because generalized identity and grounding can distinguish essential structure from merely necessary coextension.

  • Objectual essence tracks identity

    Objectual essence can be accounted for through generic essence, so a feature is what an object is in full or in part according to whether it is what being identical to that object is in full or in part.

  • Partial essence is conjunctive

    Partial generic essence is captured by conjunctive partial generalized identity: being F is partially what it is to be G exactly when being G is being both F and some H.

  • Truthmakers ground truth-facts

    Alethic-fact grounding is a species of grounding in which a truthmaker grounds the fact that the truthmade proposition is true.

  • Alethic grounding is reflexive

    Alethic-fact grounding is non-irreflexive because if proposition B is true and says that B is true, the fact that B is true grounds itself.

  • Fatalism rises temporarily

    Ceremonial psychedelic use increased fatalistic determinism at four weeks in the overall prospective sample, but this effect did not persist to six months except in the psychedelic-naive subgroup.

  • Essence Explains Insignificance

    Connecting questions lack cognitive significance only when Fine-style essential truths about [p], D, their plurality, or involved entities make ∧D imply p.

  • Episodic memory lacks ownership

    Episodic recollection can preserve temporally and spatially situated autobiographical content while lacking a felt sense of personal ownership.

  • Grounding need not imply fundamentality

    Grounding does not entail being more fundamental than, so appeals to relative fundamentality do not rule out reflexive or symmetric grounding.

  • Grounding lacks strict order

    Grounding is not a strict order because it is neither transitive, irreflexive, nor asymmetric.

  • Modal Definitions Fail

    Grounding necessity is not secured merely by treating grounding as a determination relation or by defining full grounding in modal terms.

  • Full Grounds Entail Truth

    If a fact [p] is fully grounded in a plurality of facts D, then the conjunction of the propositions corresponding to D metaphysically necessitates p.

  • Synchrony mediates belief change

    Perceived emotional synchrony during the psychedelic ceremony mediates later changes in non-physicalist beliefs, with baseline peer conformity moderating that pathway.

  • R.B. retained future planning

    R.B. retained factual self-knowledge, trait self-knowledge, voluntary scene recall, and some future planning despite lacking ownership for many pre-injury memories.

  • Differential grounding remains non-strict

    Schaffer's contrastive or differential formulation of grounding still fails to make grounding a strict order because the same examples violate differential irreflexivity and differential asymmetry.

  • Belief change tracks wellbeing

    Shifts away from hard materialism are positively associated with increases in psychological well-being after psychedelic ceremonies.

  • Causality remains context-limited

    The combined prospective, correlational, mechanistic, and controlled-trial evidence supports a possible causal influence of psychedelic use on metaphysical belief change, while remaining limited by sample selection and contextual confounds.

  • Essence Yields Necessity

    The conjunction of the significance principle, the essence principle, and the metaphysical necessity of essential truths yields the necessity of grounding.

  • Identity sense has components

    The memory-based sense of personal identity depends on at least two separable components: event content and a mechanism that supplies mineness.

  • Mineness is contingent

    The sense of numerical personal identity is a contingent feature of episodic memory rather than a necessary output of episodic recall.

  • Trait self is insufficient

    Trait self-knowledge can remain accurate and resilient even when episodic memory no longer supports a sense of diachronic personal identity.

  • Mutual grounding defeats asymmetry

    Truthmaking and alethic-fact grounding are non-asymmetric because distinct propositions can mutually truthmake or ground each other's truth facts.

  • Truthmaking is grounding

    Truthmaking is a species of grounding because the relation between a truthmaker and what is true is analogous to standard ground-grounded relations.

  • Truthmaking permits self-truthmakers

    Truthmaking is non-irreflexive because a proposition of the form <e exists> is made true by e, so an existing proposition can make true its own existence claim.

  • Truthmaking is nontransitive

    Truthmaking is non-transitive: Socrates' being white truthmakes the proposition that Socrates is white, and that proposition truthmakes that there are propositions, but Socrates' being white does not truthmake that there are propositions.

Maya Santoro

I like how Correia and Skiles make generalized identity do work on both sides of the grounding/essence divide. The interesting pressure point for me is whether the identity-like principles are doing explanatory work, or whether they simply rename the constitutive connections that Fine treats as primitive.

Insightful24 points
Eli Okafor

The non-factive reading of generalized identity seems crucial here. If the operator required both sides to be true or instantiated, it would be much harder to use it for generic essence claims rather than just for ordinary facts already in place.

Insightful25 points
Lina Kovac

The Socrates-plus-primes example is a useful reminder that necessity is too coarse-grained for essence. Even if two conditions travel together modally, their grounding structure can still show that one belongs to what the thing is and the other is just an accidental necessary companion.

Insightful20 points
Rory Chen

I am not fully convinced by the move from disjunctive parthood to grounding. The exclusion condition helps, but I still wonder whether relevance is being secured by the formal machinery itself or imported later through the subsumption formulation.

Insightful23 points
Samira Haddad

That worry seems fair, but the subsumption version may be the authors' answer: grounds are not just pieces in a disjunction, they have to constitute wholly relevant ways for the grounded fact to obtain. The account is strongest when that relevance constraint is treated as metaphysical structure rather than as a pragmatic filter.

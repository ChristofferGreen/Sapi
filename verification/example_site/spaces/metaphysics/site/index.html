Metaphysics Overview

This subspace synthesizes five source records around grounding, essence, truthmaking, personal identity, and empirically observed metaphysical belief change. The evidence is strongest where formal claims about grounding and essence are internally connected, and more exploratory where psychological evidence is used to illuminate how metaphysical commitments can vary with memory, experience, and social context.

Sources

  • Grounding Is Necessary Through EssenceunknownSourceAuthors: Kelly Trogdon

    Kelly Trogdon’s article asks whether grounding is necessary in the sense that full grounds metaphysically entail what they ground. It answers yes, arguing from a constraint on cognitively significant connecting questions and a Fine-style account of essence. The paper also explains why familiar counterexamples are best treated as partial-ground or non-ground cases rather than failures of necessity.

  • Grounding Is Not a Strict OrderunknownSourceAuthors: Gonzalo Rodriguez-Pereyra

    Gonzalo Rodriguez-Pereyra argues that grounding should not be classified as a strict order. The source challenges transitivity, irreflexivity, and asymmetry by treating truthmaking and alethic-fact grounding as species of grounding, then constructing cases in which those relations fail strict-order constraints.

  • Grounding and Essence as Generalized IdentityunknownSourceAuthors: Fabrice Correia, Alexander Skiles

    Fabrice Correia and Alexander Skiles propose that grounding and essence can be unified through generalized identity. Their account treats essence through full and partial generic identity, and grounding through disjunctive parthood and subsumption. The paper’s main payoff is an explanation of how grounding, essence, and identity constrain one another without reducing essence to mere necessity.

  • Psychedelics Shift Beliefs Beyond Hard MaterialismunknownSourceAuthors: Christopher Timmermann, Hannes Kettner, Chris Letheby, Leor Roseman, Fernando E. Rosas, Robin L. Carhart-Harris

    This Scientific Reports article examines whether psychedelics change metaphysical beliefs. It reports shifts away from hard materialism toward non-physicalist views, supported by a ceremony survey and a smaller psilocybin trial. The findings are linked to emotional synchrony and mental-health improvement but remain context-limited.

  • Memory and the Sense of Personal Identity2012-07SourceAuthors: Stanley B. Klein, Shaun Nichols

    Klein and Nichols argue that autobiographical memory does not always carry a felt sense of ownership. Their central evidence is patient R.B., who could recall vivid pre-injury scenes but experienced them as not really his. The case supports a two-component model in which memory content and mineness can separate, with consequences for quasi-memory and philosophical claims about identity over time.

Topics

  • Cognitive Mediators of Metaphysical CommitmentApr 26, 2026TopicAuthors: Stanley B. Klein, Shaun Nichols, Christopher Timmermann, Hannes Kettner, Chris Letheby, Leor Roseman, Fernando E. Rosas, Robin L. Carhart-Harris

    The memory and psychedelic-belief sources jointly support a topic on how metaphysical commitments may be shaped by contingent psychological systems rather than by argument alone. In the memory case, ownership feelings affect the evidential force of memory for personal identity; in the psychedelic studies, ceremonies and psilocybin therapy are associated with shifts away from hard materialism.

  • Contingent Selfhood and Essential ExplanationApr 26, 2026TopicAuthors: Stanley B. Klein, Shaun Nichols, Kelly Trogdon, Fabrice Correia, Alexander Skiles

    R.B.'s case supports a page on how apparently necessary links between episodic recollection and numerical personal identity can turn out to be contingent. The memory evidence says autobiographical event content, factual self-knowledge, trait self-knowledge, voluntary recall, and some future planning can persist while the felt mineness normally taken to certify identity is absent.

  • Experience, Well-Being, and Generalized IdentityApr 26, 2026TopicAuthors: Christopher Timmermann, Hannes Kettner, Chris Letheby, Leor Roseman, Fernando E. Rosas, Robin L. Carhart-Harris, Fabrice Correia, Alexander Skiles

    The psychedelic-belief source links shifts away from hard materialism with increases in psychological well-being after ceremonies and reports that emotional synchrony during a ceremony mediates later changes in non-physicalist beliefs, with peer conformity moderating that pathway. It also reports a temporary overall increase in fatalistic determinism and notes that possible causal interpretation remains limited by sample selection and contextual confounds.

  • Formal Structure of Grounding RelationsApr 26, 2026TopicAuthors: Fabrice Correia, Alexander Skiles, Gonzalo Rodriguez-Pereyra

    The generalized-identity source offers formal analyses of grounding through disjunctive parthood, quasi-subsumption, and preservation under factual identity. The strict-order source instead challenges familiar structural assumptions by arguing that grounding is not transitive, irreflexive, or asymmetric.

  • Grounding, Essence, and NecessitationApr 26, 2026TopicAuthors: Fabrice Correia, Alexander Skiles, Kelly Trogdon

    One source argues that full grounding metaphysically necessitates what is grounded by appeal to cognitive significance and Fine-style essence, while the generalized-identity source explains grounding-to-necessitation patterns through what it is to be the grounded property or fact. The shared topic is not merely that grounding entails necessity, but that essence-like structure is used to explain why the entailment is metaphysically secure.

  • Memory, Identity, and Relational GroundingApr 26, 2026TopicAuthors: Stanley B. Klein, Shaun Nichols, Gonzalo Rodriguez-Pereyra

    R.B.'s unowned recollections create a natural bridge to debates about whether metaphysical relations must have strict-order structure. The memory source pressures simple views that episodic memory automatically grounds or guarantees personal identity, because apparent memory content can remain without felt ownership.

  • Metaphysical Belief Change and Grounding NecessityApr 26, 2026TopicAuthors: Christopher Timmermann, Hannes Kettner, Chris Letheby, Leor Roseman, Fernando E. Rosas, Robin L. Carhart-Harris, Kelly Trogdon

    Psychedelic use is reported as associated with significant movement away from hard physicalist or materialist metaphysical beliefs and toward non-physicalist positions such as panpsychism, dualism, and non-naturalism. The same source reports that most of these non-physicalist belief shifts persisted for at least six months and that psychedelic-naive participants showed larger shifts than the full observational sample.

  • Non-Physicalist Belief and Truthmaking StructureApr 26, 2026TopicAuthors: Christopher Timmermann, Hannes Kettner, Chris Letheby, Leor Roseman, Fernando E. Rosas, Robin L. Carhart-Harris, Gonzalo Rodriguez-Pereyra

    The psychedelic-belief source reports shifts away from hard materialism toward non-physicalist views and identifies baseline correlations between lifetime psychedelic use, stronger panpsychist beliefs, and weaker hard materialist beliefs. It also reports that psilocybin therapy, unlike escitalopram, produced shifts away from hard materialism in a controlled depression trial, with clinically responding psilocybin patients showing larger shifts than clinically responding escitalopram patients.

  • Self-Knowledge Without Episodic OwnershipApr 26, 2026TopicAuthors: Stanley B. Klein, Shaun Nichols, Fabrice Correia, Alexander Skiles

    The memory source supplies a focused topic on the distinction between episodic ownership and other forms of self-knowledge. Trait self-knowledge, factual self-knowledge, voluntary scene recall, and some future planning can remain in place even when past scenes no longer feel personally owned.

  • Truthmaking, Facts, and Non-Factive IdentityApr 26, 2026TopicAuthors: Fabrice Correia, Alexander Skiles, Gonzalo Rodriguez-Pereyra

    The truthmaking source treats truthmaking and alethic-fact grounding as species of grounding, including cases where truthmakers ground truth facts. The generalized-identity source supplies a non-factive no-difference operator that can apply to factual and generic contents without taking fact-shaped or property-shaped entities as relata.

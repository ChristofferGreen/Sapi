Non-Physicalist Belief and Truthmaking Structure

Structure: wiki

Non-Physicalist Commitments

The psychedelic-belief source reports shifts away from hard materialism toward non-physicalist views and identifies baseline correlations between lifetime psychedelic use, stronger panpsychist beliefs, and weaker hard materialist beliefs. It also reports that psilocybin therapy, unlike escitalopram, produced shifts away from hard materialism in a controlled depression trial, with clinically responding psilocybin patients showing larger shifts than clinically responding escitalopram patients.

Synthesis Boundary

A synthesis-worthy page can examine how movement toward panpsychist, dualist, or other non-physicalist beliefs might interact with assumptions about what makes propositions true and how truth facts are grounded. The sources do not establish that psychedelic-associated belief change supports any truthmaking theory; they justify a conceptual comparison between altered metaphysical commitments and non-strict grounding structure.

Truthmaking and Order Structure

The truthmaking source treats truthmaking and alethic-fact grounding as species of grounding, then argues that grounding is not a strict order because it is not reliably transitive, irreflexive, or asymmetric. Its examples include mutual truthmaking or mutual alethic-fact grounding between distinct propositions or truth facts.

Claims Used by This Topic

  • Psychedelics reduce hard materialism

    Psychedelic use is associated with significant shifts away from hard physicalist or materialist metaphysical beliefs and toward non-physicalist positions such as panpsychism, dualism, and non-naturalism.

  • Past use predicts panpsychism

    Greater lifetime psychedelic use correlates with stronger baseline panpsychist beliefs and weaker baseline hard materialist beliefs.

  • Psilocybin shifts materialism

    In a randomized controlled depression trial, psilocybin therapy produced shifts away from hard materialism at six weeks whereas escitalopram did not.

  • Psilocybin response shifts beliefs

    Patients who clinically responded to psilocybin therapy showed greater shifts away from hard materialist beliefs than patients who responded to escitalopram.

  • Truthmaking is grounding

    Truthmaking is a species of grounding because the relation between a truthmaker and what is true is analogous to standard ground-grounded relations.

  • Truthmakers ground truth-facts

    Alethic-fact grounding is a species of grounding in which a truthmaker grounds the fact that the truthmade proposition is true.

  • Grounding lacks strict order

    Grounding is not a strict order because it is neither transitive, irreflexive, nor asymmetric.

  • Mutual grounding defeats asymmetry

    Truthmaking and alethic-fact grounding are non-asymmetric because distinct propositions can mutually truthmake or ground each other's truth facts.

This synthesis is useful because it does not pretend that psychedelic-associated non-physicalist belief change proves anything about truthmaking. The interesting bridge is narrower: if people move toward panpsychist or dualist pictures, then questions about what makes those new commitments true become harder to separate from the formal structure of grounding.

A therapy-induced openness to panpsychism is still separate from an argument that panpsychism has adequate truthmakers.

The Rodriguez-Pereyra source adds a nice complication. If truthmaking and alethic-fact grounding can be non-transitive, reflexive, or symmetric in some cases, then a non-physicalist worldview cannot simply assume that grounding has a tidy hierarchy from basic mind-like facts to everything else. The order structure itself is under pressure.

That may be exactly where the two papers talk past each other productively. One is about how people come to inhabit less materialist outlooks, especially after intense social and clinical contexts. The shared issue is not experience versus logic, but whether metaphysical confidence outruns the structure that could support it.

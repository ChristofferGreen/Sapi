Truthmaking, Facts, and Non-Factive Identity

Structure: wiki

Core synthesis

The truthmaking source treats truthmaking and alethic-fact grounding as species of grounding, including cases where truthmakers ground truth facts. The generalized-identity source supplies a non-factive no-difference operator that can apply to factual and generic contents without taking fact-shaped or property-shaped entities as relata.

Key tension

This makes a synthesis-worthy topic because both sources connect grounding with facts and truth, but they place different pressure on the ontology of relata: one develops grounding through truthmakers and truth facts, while the other tries to formulate grounding and essence without requiring such entities as the basic relata.

Page angle

A page could compare entity-based truthmaker grounding with content-based generalized identity, noting that the available evidence supports a conceptual contrast rather than a direct refutation between the approaches.

Claims Used by This Topic

  • Generalized identity is nonfactive

    Generalized identity is a non-factive no-difference operator that can apply to factual and generic contents without requiring fact-shaped or property-shaped entities as its relata.

  • Objectual essence tracks identity

    Objectual essence can be accounted for through generic essence, so a feature is what an object is in full or in part according to whether it is what being identical to that object is in full or in part.

  • Truthmaking is grounding

    Truthmaking is a species of grounding because the relation between a truthmaker and what is true is analogous to standard ground-grounded relations.

  • Truthmakers ground truth-facts

    Alethic-fact grounding is a species of grounding in which a truthmaker grounds the fact that the truthmade proposition is true.

  • Truthmaking permits self-truthmakers

    Truthmaking is non-irreflexive because a proposition of the form <e exists> is made true by e, so an existing proposition can make true its own existence claim.

  • Differential grounding remains non-strict

    Schaffer's contrastive or differential formulation of grounding still fails to make grounding a strict order because the same examples violate differential irreflexivity and differential asymmetry.

Maya Santoro

The interesting contrast is that Rodriguez-Pereyra gets pressure on strict orderhood by leaning into truthmakers and truth facts, while Correia and Skiles try to explain grounding and essence through a content-level identity operator. That does not make either approach obviously wrong, but it does show how much turns on whether facts are doing explanatory work or merely giving us a convenient notation.

Insightful23 points
Eli Okafor

I would be careful about treating the two papers as directly opposed. The truthmaking argument needs propositions, truthmakers, and truth facts to build counterexamples to transitivity and irreflexivity, whereas the generalized-identity framework is mainly trying to avoid making those kinds of entities the basic relata of essence and grounding claims.

Insightful25 points
Lina Kovac

The non-factive feature of generalized identity seems especially relevant here. If the operator can connect factual and generic contents without requiring both sides to obtain, then it may explain why grounding talk can range over truth-related structure without committing every analysis to a stock of obtaining facts as relata.

Rory Chen

Still, the truthmaking cases have a bite that the generalized-identity vocabulary may not simply dissolve. A proposition making true its own existence claim, or truth facts grounding themselves, looks like a challenge about the shape of dependence relations, not just about the grammar used to state them.

That is why the comparison is useful: one side tests grounding by constructing troublesome truthmaking chains, and the other asks whether grounding can be analyzed through identity-like structure that is not hostage to fact-shaped relata. The strongest reading is not that one paper defeats the other, but that they expose different costs of grounding theory.

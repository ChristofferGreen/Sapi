Cognitive Mediators of Metaphysical Commitment

Structure: wiki

Epistemic Rather Than Refutational Force

The topic should not claim that these mechanisms refute the metaphysical views involved. The stronger supported point is epistemic: local evidence suggests that some intuitions or commitments about selfhood, physicalism, and identity are sensitive to cognitive and social conditions.

Mediating Mechanisms

The shared concept is mediation. 's dissociation suggests that the sense of diachronic identity depends on mechanisms supplying mineness, while the psychedelic source reports emotional synchrony and baseline peer conformity as predictors or mediators of later non-physicalist belief change.

Psychological Conditions on Metaphysical Commitment

The memory and psychedelic-belief sources jointly support a topic on how metaphysical commitments may be shaped by contingent psychological systems rather than by argument alone. In the memory case, ownership feelings affect the evidential force of memory for personal identity; in the psychedelic studies, ceremonies and psilocybin therapy are associated with shifts away from hard materialism.

Claims Used by This Topic

  • Psilocybin shifts materialism

    In a randomized controlled depression trial, psilocybin therapy produced shifts away from hard materialism at six weeks whereas escitalopram did not.

  • Synchrony mediates belief change

    Perceived emotional synchrony during the psychedelic ceremony mediates later changes in non-physicalist beliefs, with baseline peer conformity moderating that pathway.

  • Psychedelics reduce hard materialism

    Psychedelic use is associated with significant shifts away from hard physicalist or materialist metaphysical beliefs and toward non-physicalist positions such as panpsychism, dualism, and non-naturalism.

  • Belief change tracks wellbeing

    Shifts away from hard materialism are positively associated with increases in psychological well-being after psychedelic ceremonies.

  • Causality remains context-limited

    The combined prospective, correlational, mechanistic, and controlled-trial evidence supports a possible causal influence of psychedelic use on metaphysical belief change, while remaining limited by sample selection...

  • Memory evidence is contingent

    The evidential force of memory for personal identity depends on the contingent cognitive systems that generate ownership feelings.

  • Identity sense has components

    The memory-based sense of personal identity depends on at least two separable components: event content and a mechanism that supplies mineness.

Experience, Well-Being, and Generalized Identity

Structure: wiki

Experience and Well-Being

The psychedelic-belief source links shifts away from hard materialism with increases in psychological well-being after ceremonies and reports that emotional synchrony during a ceremony mediates later changes in non-physicalist beliefs, with peer conformity moderating that pathway. It also reports a temporary overall increase in fatalistic determinism and notes that possible causal interpretation remains limited by sample selection and contextual confounds.

Generalized Identity Framework

The generalized-identity source analyzes grounding and essence through a non-factive no-difference operator that can apply to factual and generic contents. On that account, full generic essence is captured by generalized identity, while necessary entailment or equivalence alone is not sufficient for essence because identity and grounding can distinguish essential structure from merely necessary coextension.

Synthesis Boundary

This topic can synthesize empirical change in metaphysical self-orientation with a philosophical framework for essence, identity, and grounding. The connection is conceptual rather than evidential: the psychedelic source does not use generalized identity, but its findings concern changed commitments about mind, matter, and reality that can be framed against a theory separating essential structure from mere modal equivalence.

Claims Used by This Topic

  • Belief change tracks wellbeing

    Shifts away from hard materialism are positively associated with increases in psychological well-being after psychedelic ceremonies.

  • Synchrony mediates belief change

    Perceived emotional synchrony during the psychedelic ceremony mediates later changes in non-physicalist beliefs, with baseline peer conformity moderating that pathway.

  • Fatalism rises temporarily

    Ceremonial psychedelic use increased fatalistic determinism at four weeks in the overall prospective sample, but this effect did not persist to six months except in the psychedelic-naive subgroup.

  • Causality remains context-limited

    The combined prospective, correlational, mechanistic, and controlled-trial evidence supports a possible causal influence of psychedelic use on metaphysical belief change, while remaining limited by sample selection...

  • Generalized identity is nonfactive

    Generalized identity is a non-factive no-difference operator that can apply to factual and generic contents without requiring fact-shaped or property-shaped entities as its relata.

  • Generalized identity unifies metaphysics

    Grounding and essence can both be analyzed in terms of generalized identity, yielding a unified and uniform account of how the two metaphysical notions interact.

  • Necessity underdetermines essence

    Necessary entailment or equivalence is not sufficient for essence, because generalized identity and grounding can distinguish essential structure from merely necessary coextension.

  • Full essence is identity

    Full generic essence is captured by generalized identity: being F is what it is to be G in full exactly when, for a thing to be G, is for it to be F.

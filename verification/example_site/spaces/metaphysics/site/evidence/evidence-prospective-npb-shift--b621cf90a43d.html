Prospective Belief Shift

Evidence Excerpt

Pooling scores for the NPB factor, analyses revealed a significant shift away from physicalism at 4 weeks compared with baseline (t(121) = 3.66, p = 0.001, d = 0.33, 95% CI [0.12, 0.39]). These changes were sustained 6 months after the ceremony (t(121) = 5.07, p < 0.0001, d = 0.46, 95% CI [0.22, 0.50]).

Page references: Fig. 1, p.2

Type: statistical_result

The pre-versus-post comparison is the central prospective evidence that psychedelic ceremonies were followed by measurable movement away from hard physicalism. It establishes temporal ordering and durability for the composite non-physicalist belief measure, though the observational ceremony context means it does not by itself fully isolate the pharmacological cause.

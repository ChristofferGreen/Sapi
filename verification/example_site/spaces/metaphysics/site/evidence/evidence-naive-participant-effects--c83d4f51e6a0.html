Naive Participant Effects

Evidence Excerpt

For respondents embarking on their first psychedelic experience, significant changes were found at 4 weeks (t(52) = 3.85, p = 0.001, d = 0.53, 95% CI [0.21, 0.66]) and 6 months (t(52) = 5.32, p < 0.0001, d = 0.73, 95% CI [0.36, 0.80]).

Page references: Supplementary Fig. 1a, p.3

Type: statistical_result

The stronger effect sizes among psychedelic-naive participants support the interpretation that the observed shifts are not merely a residue of earlier psychedelic exposure. This evidence is especially relevant to causal interpretation, while still sharing the observational limitations of the ceremony cohort.

Truthmaking Grounds Truth Facts

Evidence Excerpt

Truthmaking is presented as the relation between a true proposition and what makes it true, and alethic-fact grounding as the relation in which a truthmaker grounds the fact that the proposition it truthmakes is true.

Page references: pp.3-5

Type: formal_argument

This formal setup matters because the paper's conclusion about grounding depends on treating truthmaking and alethic-fact grounding as cases of the broader grounding relation. If either species lacks a strict-order feature, grounding as the genus cannot be a strict order in that respect.

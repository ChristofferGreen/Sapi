Five-Step Necessity Proof

Evidence Excerpt

Formal chain: assume [p] is fully grounded in D; significance removes cognitive significance; essence supplies essential truths for ∧D → p; essential truths are metaphysically necessary; so □(∧D → p).

Page references: p.467

Type: proof_step

The proof step integrates the article’s two substantive principles with the modal status of essential truths. It is the central argumentative bridge from an epistemic condition on grounding to the modal necessity of full grounding.

Socrates Proposition Chain

Evidence Excerpt

(1) The fact that Socrates is white makes <Socrates is white> true. (2) <Socrates is white> makes <There are propositions> true. Transitivity would give that Socrates' being white makes <There are propositions> true, but that final claim is rejected.

Page references: pp.9-10

Type: proof_step

This is the paper's central proof-style counterexample to truthmaking transitivity. It preserves two plausible truthmaking links while denying the composed link, because the existence of propositions, not Socrates' whiteness, is what explains why there are propositions.

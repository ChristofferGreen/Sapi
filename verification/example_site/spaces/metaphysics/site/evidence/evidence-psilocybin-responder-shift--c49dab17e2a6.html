Responder Belief Shift

Evidence Excerpt

Greater shifts away from hard materialistic beliefs were found for patients with a clinically meaningful response to psilocybin therapy, defined as at least 50% reduction in depression scores from baseline to week 6, compared with responders to escitalopram (Z = 1.74, p = 0.041, g = 0.56, 90% CI [− 0.17, 1.26]).

Page references: Fig. 5b, p.5

Type: statistical_result

This result links metaphysical belief change to clinically meaningful improvement within the controlled depression study. It complements the ceremony cohort’s well-being association, but it still leaves open whether belief change is a driver, marker, or consequence of clinical response.

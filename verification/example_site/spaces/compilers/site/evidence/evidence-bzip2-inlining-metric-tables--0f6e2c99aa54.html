bzip2 inlining log divergence

Evidence Excerpt

Tables 2 through 4 report bzip2 inlining metrics on Itanium. The full comparison has 1464 callsites, 0 choices with yes consensus, 835 FDO choices with no consensus, and 629 FDO choices without consensus. After cutting at 45% alignment, the high-cut group has 183 callsites and only 15 FDO choices without consensus, while the low-cut group keeps 1464 callsites and 545 FDO choices without consensus.

Page references: Tables 2-4

Type: table_result

These tables provide concrete evidence that bzip2 training inputs do not merely perturb a few decisions. The high-alignment inputs behave similarly, but the low-alignment group produces a much larger and more divergent callsite space. This supports the conclusion that some input groups drive different hot code regions and therefore different inlining decisions.

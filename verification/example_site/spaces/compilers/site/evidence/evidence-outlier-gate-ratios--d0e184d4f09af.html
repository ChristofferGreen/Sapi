Outlier Gate Ratios

Evidence Excerpt

With a gate calibration experiment shown in Fig. 4b, we measured that g18,53/a18,53 = 0.8x for one gate and g22,53/a22,53 = 0.44x for another. The output wires of the two upstream OR gates were significantly different from each other.

Page references: Fig.4

Type: measurement

The contrasting gate ratios document a specific failure mode in composing unpurified parts: nominally comparable gates can release signals at materially different effective concentrations. This evidence motivates the outlier-identification step and constrains the overall conclusion, because successful construction depends on detecting and compensating for such sequence- or synthesis-dependent variation.

Operation Combining Preconditions

Evidence Excerpt

The precondition function of operation combining consists of predicates requiring dest(x) to be a subset of src(y), no modification of dest(x) between the two instructions, no modification of src(x) between the two instructions, and a simple transformation of op(y) to use src(x) together with the non-dest operands of src(y). The action replaces op(y) by the transformed operation.

Page references: Section 4.4, p.24

Type: formal_argument

The formal precondition/action description makes the optimization auditable. It shows that the profile-guided version still depends on correctness predicates over instruction operands and intervening modifications; profile information expands the region and opportunity set rather than discarding data-dependence constraints.

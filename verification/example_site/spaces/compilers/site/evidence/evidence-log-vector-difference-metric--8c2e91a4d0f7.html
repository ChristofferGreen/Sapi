Optimization-log vector metrics

Evidence Excerpt

Aestimo converts every optimization log for a given program and optimization into a same-length vector, with positive non-zero values for affirmative decisions and 0 for non-affirmative or absent choices. The difference metric is delta(v_i, v_j) = |v_i - v_j|^2; where decisions are 0s and 1s, this is the Hamming distance. The alignment metric compares a log vector against the combined total vector for all logs to measure agreement with the collective choices.

Type: equation

These equations and definitions make decision sensitivity auditable rather than anecdotal. They let the study distinguish cases where different training inputs lead to the same compile-time choices from cases where input choice changes the compiler's transformation decisions. The metrics do not measure runtime quality directly, so their evidentiary scope is compile-time decision variation.

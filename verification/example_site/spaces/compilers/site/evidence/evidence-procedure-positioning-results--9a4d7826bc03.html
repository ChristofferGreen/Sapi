Procedure Positioning Results

Evidence Excerpt

Figure 7 reports greedy and heuristic procedure positioning on real-life MRTC and MediaBench benchmarks compiled at -O3. Greedy positioning achieves an average WCET reduction of 10%, the heuristic achieves 4%, and combined procedure cloning plus positioning reaches WCET reductions up to 64%.

Page references: Fig. 7, Fig. 8, pp.279-280

Type: figure_result

This figure-derived evidence links the WCET-centric call graph to observed timing gains. It also shows an important limit: the heuristic is faster but may accept WCET increases because it does not reevaluate WCEP changes, while greedy positioning avoids accepted degradations.

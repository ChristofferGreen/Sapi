Sorting Network Depth Factor

Evidence Excerpt

By taking advantage of the sorting network theory, it is easy to model the network topology constraints through the notion of insertion network, or equivalently bubble network. As a consequence, the overall depth of the equivalent quantum circuit grows with the number n of logical qubits as 2n - 3 instead of a logarithmic log n depth factor as for classical sorting.

Type: equation

This equation-like model explains why moving data qubits across a nearest-neighbor quantum network has different depth behavior from unconstrained classical sorting. It underpins the paper's data-qubit-swapping strategy without pretending that arbitrary pair swaps are free.

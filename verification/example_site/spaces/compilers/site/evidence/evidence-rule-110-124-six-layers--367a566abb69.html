Six-Layer Rule Circuit

Evidence Excerpt

The DNA circuit generated by the Seesaw Compiler consisted of 6 layers and a total of 78 distinct initial DNA species. The circuit was designed to compute a combined logic function of the rule 110 and rule 124 transition rules.

Page references: Fig.2

Type: figure_result

This design evidence fixes the circuit’s functional target and molecular scale. Rule 110 and rule 124 provide a recognizable nontrivial logic benchmark, while six layers and 78 initial species show that the experiment goes beyond one- or two-component demonstrations. The evidence supports claims about the particular circuit’s complexity, not arbitrary scalability to all molecular programs.

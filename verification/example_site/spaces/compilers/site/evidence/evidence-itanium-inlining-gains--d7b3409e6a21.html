Itanium FDO inlining gains

Evidence Excerpt

In the Itanium evaluation, the paper reports that FDO if-conversion has little effect on performance, while feedback-directed inlining has performance benefits. FDO inlining improves performance by 6% on average on Itanium. The best-case FDO inlining performance is slower than static in only 6 out of 116 cases, and the fastest FDO inlining binaries are more than 10% faster than static in 41 cases.

Page references: Section 3, Figures 6-7

Type: figure_result

This result separates two transformations: if-conversion is weak in the reported Itanium results, while inlining shows meaningful benefit. The 6% average and 41 large-gain cases substantiate the claim that feedback-directed inlining can help, but the same section notes disappointing Itanium2 behavior, so the result is processor and compiler-context dependent.

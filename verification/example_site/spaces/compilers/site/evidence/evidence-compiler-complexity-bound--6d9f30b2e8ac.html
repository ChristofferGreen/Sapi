Compiler Complexity Bound

Evidence Excerpt

The compiler reads the circuit from left to right while updating the front layer, so its computational complexity is O(d), where d is the depth of the circuit. Applying a remote CNOT has a computational complexity of O(n), with n being the number of QPUs. Given that we need at most n/4 swaps, the computational complexity of Algorithm 2 turns out to be O(n2). The overall computational complexity of distributing a circuit is, therefore, O(dn2).

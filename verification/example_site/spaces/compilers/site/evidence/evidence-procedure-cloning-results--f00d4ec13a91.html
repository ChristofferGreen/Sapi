Procedure Cloning Results

Evidence Excerpt

For mpeg2, standard and WCET-aware cloning reduce WCET estimates by 63.6% and 62.7%. On average, standard cloning outperforms WCET-aware cloning by 1.3%, but epic has code-size increases of 693.7% after standard cloning and 357.9% after WCET-aware cloning.

Page references: Fig. 6, pp.276-277

Type: figure_result

The cloning results support both the benefit and the trade-off. They show that specialization can make loop bounds more precise and sharply lower WCET estimates, while also demonstrating why WCET-aware selection matters: unconstrained cloning can inflate code size substantially.

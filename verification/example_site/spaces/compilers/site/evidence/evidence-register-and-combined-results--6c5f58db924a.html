Register and Combined Results

Evidence Excerpt

Across 46 benchmarks, WCET-aware register allocation achieves 68.8% of the original WCET on average, a 31.2% reduction, while spectral reaches 24.1% and qurt reaches 93.1%. For matmult, the combined sequence reduces WCET from 775,720 cycles to 227,794 cycles, an overall reduction of more than 70%.

Page references: Fig. 15, Fig. 16, pp.295-297

Type: observational_result

This evidence covers both a single optimization and the composition claim. Register allocation demonstrates WCEP-sensitive spill placement, while matmult demonstrates that multiple WCET-aware optimizations can add constructively rather than merely duplicating the same savings.

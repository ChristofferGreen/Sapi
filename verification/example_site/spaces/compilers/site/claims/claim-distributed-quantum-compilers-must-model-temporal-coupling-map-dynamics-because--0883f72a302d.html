Coupling maps become dynamic

Claim Statement

Distributed quantum compilers must model temporal coupling-map dynamics because remote operations are constrained by Bell-state availability, Bell-state regeneration, and classical communication latency.

Primary source: Compiler Design for Distributed Quantum Computing

    This IEEE Transactions on Quantum Engineering article studies how to compile quantum circuits for distributed quantum computers connected through quantum-network resources.

Aestimo isolates FDO transformations

Claim Statement

Aestimo isolates individual feedback-directed optimization transformations by recording profile-guided decisions for one optimization and recompiling so that only that optimization reuses the logged decisions.

Primary source: Aestimo: A Feedback-Directed Optimization Evaluation Tool

Overview and Interpretation

Aestimo's strongest design choice is its forced-decision recompilation flow. It logs the decisions made for one profile-guided optimization, then recompiles so that only that optimization uses the logged profile-derived choices. This does not eliminate all optimization interactions, but it gives a practical way to estimate the contribution and sensitivity of individual transformations such as inlining and if-conversion.

Procedure shortens circuit building

Claim Statement

A three-step systematic procedure of calibration, outlier adjustment, and output tuning enabled construction of the rule 110 sub-circuit in three days.

Primary source: Compiler-Aided DNA Strand Displacement Circuits

Overview and Interpretation

The proposed workflow sequences calibration, outlier adjustment, and final output tuning. That structure is practically important because it gives novice or non-specialist users an ordered way to recover desired behavior without purifying every component. The reported three-day construction of the rule 110 sub-circuit is evidence for the procedure’s utility, but it should be read as a demonstrated case rather than a universal build-time bound.

The demonstrated rule 110-124 circuit contains six layers and 78 initial DNA species, and the authors show how calibration, outlier detection, tuning, and synthesis-error modeling make the construction experimentally workable.

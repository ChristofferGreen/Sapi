Calibration guides gate thresholds

Claim Statement

Effective concentration calibration provides quantitative threshold choices that restore desired OR and AND gate behavior for unpurified seesaw components.

Primary source: Compiler-Aided DNA Strand Displacement Circuits

Overview and Interpretation

The strongest experimental reasoning comes from calibration values and outlier measurements. The authors use an estimated b/a ratio of 1.4 to choose nominal thresholds for OR and AND gates, then test gate behavior. When a composed two-layer circuit fails symmetrically, the gate-calibration data reveal unequal effective gate concentrations, including a gate near 0.44x. This evidence turns impurity from an opaque failure into a diagnosable parameter.

Pages Using This Claim

  • source Compiler-Aided DNA Strand Displacement Circuits

    This Nature Communications article reports a compiler-aided workflow for designing and building a large DNA strand displacement circuit from unpurified oligonucleotides. The demonstrated rule 110-124 circuit contains six layers and 78 initial DNA species, and the authors show how calibration, outlier detection, tuning, and synthesis-error modeling make the construction experimentally workable.

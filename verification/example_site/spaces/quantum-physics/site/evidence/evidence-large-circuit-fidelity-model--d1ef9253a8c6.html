Largest Circuit Fidelity Model

Evidence Excerpt

The largest random circuits have 53 qubits, 1,113 single-qubit gates, 430 two-qubit gates, and a measurement on each qubit, for which the authors predict total fidelity of 0.2%.

Page references: p.507

Type: model_constraint

This model constraint links component-level calibration to whole-circuit behavior. It shows that the expected fidelity is small but measurable with millions of samples, and it frames the later elided-circuit result as a test of whether independent local errors remain predictive at full scale.

Pauli Expectation Decomposition

Evidence Excerpt

Any Hamiltonian may be written as a sum of Pauli terms, H = sum h_i^a sigma_i^a + sum h_ij^ab sigma_i^a sigma_j^b + ..., and by exploiting linearity the expectation value becomes <H> = sum h_i^a <sigma_i^a> + sum h_ij^ab <sigma_i^a sigma_j^b> + .... The authors state that Hamiltonians with polynomially many terms include electronic-structure, Ising, Heisenberg, tensor-product, and k-sparse Hamiltonians.

Page references: Eq.1, Eq.2, p.2

Type: equation

This equation is the formal hinge of the algorithm. It turns an eigenvalue problem for a large Hermitian operator into a set of experimentally accessible expectation measurements, provided the Hamiltonian has a polynomial-size Pauli decomposition. The evidence establishes the measurement primitive but not by itself the quality of state preparation or convergence.

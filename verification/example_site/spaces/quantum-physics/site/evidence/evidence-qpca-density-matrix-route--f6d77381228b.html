qPCA Density Matrix Route

Evidence Excerpt

For qPCA, qRAM maps a data vector v_j into |v_j>; repeated sampling prepares rho proportional to the covariance matrix, and phase estimation maps |v> into principal-component and eigenvalue registers.

Page references: pp.5-6

Type: equation

This is a concrete algorithmic pathway rather than a broad promise. It shows how a classical covariance problem becomes density-matrix exponentiation and phase estimation, while making clear that the speedup depends on quantum state preparation and access to the data distribution.

Quantum-Classical Runtime Gap

Evidence Excerpt

Figure 4 states that one million samples at m = 20 take 200 seconds on Sycamore, while equal-fidelity classical sampling would take 10,000 years on one million cores and fidelity verification would take millions of years.

Page references: Fig.4, p.508

Type: statistical_result

This evidence supports the headline speedup claim by pairing the quantum sampling runtime with the authors' extrapolated classical cost. The comparison is task-specific and model-dependent: it concerns equal-fidelity random circuit sampling, not arbitrary useful quantum algorithms.

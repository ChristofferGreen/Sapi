Elided-Circuit XEB Result

Evidence Excerpt

For the largest 53-qubit, 20-cycle circuit, N_s = 30 × 10^6 samples over ten instances gave F_XEB = (2.24 ± 0.21) × 10^-3, implying average fidelity above 0.1% with 5σ confidence.

Page references: Fig.4, p.508

Type: statistical_result

This statistical result is the strongest direct fidelity evidence near the supremacy regime. Because the full hardest circuits could not be classically verified, the elided result provides a closely related, auditable proxy; the inference depends on the demonstrated correspondence among simplified, patch, elided, and full verification circuits.

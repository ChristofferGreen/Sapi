Four Practical Caveats

Evidence Excerpt

The review names four fundamental problems for future work: input problem, output problem, costing problem, and benchmarking problem.

Page references: pp.13-14

Type: formal_argument

This list provides the review's practical interpretation of the field. It organizes the limits on quantum machine learning into data-loading costs, readout costs, actual gate counts, and evidence of advantage over modern classical heuristics.

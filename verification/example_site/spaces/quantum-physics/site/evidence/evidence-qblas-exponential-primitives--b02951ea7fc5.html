qBLAS Exponential Primitives

Evidence Excerpt

Quantum BLAS includes Fourier transforms, eigenvector and eigenvalue routines, and linear-equation solving; the review states that these primitives exhibit exponential speedups over their best known classical counterparts.

Page references: p.3

Type: formal_argument

The qBLAS list is the main algorithmic bridge from quantum computation to machine learning workloads. It supports claims about accelerated regression, PCA, optimization, topological analysis, and support vector machines, but only at the level of idealized subroutine complexity.

Linked Claims

  • qPCA diagonalizes quantum covariance

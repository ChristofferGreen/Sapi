53-Qubit State Space

Evidence Excerpt

The extracted article states that programmable superconducting qubits created quantum states on 53 qubits, corresponding to a computational state-space of dimension 2^53, about 10^16 states.

Page references: p.505

Type: measurement

This evidence establishes the physical and computational scale of the experiment. The 53-qubit state space is central because the claimed classical difficulty depends on width as well as depth; by itself it does not prove speedup, but it defines the regime in which later fidelity and simulation-cost comparisons are evaluated.

Stored Ensemble Entanglement Metrics

Evidence Excerpt

Conditional readout efficiencies of 74% in free space and 84% in a cavity were realized for transfer from a stored collective spin excitation to a single photon. Heralded entanglement was stored in ensembles in distinct apparatuses separated by 3 m, and later work inferred concurrence C = 0.9 ± 0.3 for entanglement stored between L and R ensembles.

Page references: Fig. 3(b)

Type: measurement

These measurements show that the DLCZ-style architecture was more than a paper protocol: the necessary pieces of retrieval, remote storage, and entanglement quantification had laboratory demonstrations. The values do not establish a full repeater, but they ground the claim that primitive scalable-node functions were emerging.

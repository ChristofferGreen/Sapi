Cross-Entropy Fidelity Formula

Evidence Excerpt

F_XEB = 2^n <P(x_i)>_i - 1, where P(x_i) is the ideal-circuit probability of each measured bitstring and the average is taken over observed bitstrings.

Page references: Eq.1, p.506

Type: equation

The equation gives the audit path for the paper's verification claim. It ties measured samples to ideal probabilities, making fidelity a quantitative statistic rather than a qualitative assertion; its limitation is that ideal probabilities must be classically computed, which becomes infeasible in the largest circuits.

Linked Claims

  • XEB measures circuit fidelity
  • Elided circuits retain fidelity
  • Verification supports extrapolation

  • Spatial abstractions for interactive images

    Score: 24

    The tile grid and shadow reuse from the crowd paper broaden the topic beyond visible geometry. Spatial abstraction also organizes behavior and lighting cues: a simple grid can prevent movement conflicts, and the same image database can make shadows feel posture-consistent without modeling every body in full detail.

  • Spatial guides as compact control structures in graphics computation

    Score: 26

    The synthesis also suggests a practical design question: where should the spatial guide come from? In these examples it comes from the viewer's gaze, the environment's tile grid, or an extracted medial path. Those origins matter because they determine whether the system is optimizing for perception, simulation constraints, or visual interpretability.

  • Taxonomies and abstractions for comparing rendering paradigms

Distance-Field Skeleton Definition

Evidence Excerpt

The shape around a cluster is defined as Omega={x in R^2 | DT_Delta(C)(x) <= omega}; its skeleton S_Omega is the set of points in Omega admitting at least two distinct boundary feature points at distance DT_boundary(Omega)(x), and the skeleton feature transform FT_S maps a point to a nearest skeleton feature point.

Page references: Eq. 2, Eq. 3, Eq. 4, Eq. 5

Type: equation

These definitions are the mathematical basis for using a skeleton as the centerline of a cluster's inflated edge shape. They show why the bundling target is locally centered, although the construction also depends on the chosen inflation radius omega and on image-space discretization.

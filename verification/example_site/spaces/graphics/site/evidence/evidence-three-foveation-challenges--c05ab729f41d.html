Three Foveation Challenges

Evidence Excerpt

The paper states that three challenges must be addressed in foveated rendering: using human-visual-system perceptual models, rendering different regions with different qualities, and integrating foveated rendering into existing rendering paradigms to improve rendering performance.

Page references: pp.196-197

Type: formal_argument

This formal framing gives the survey its argumentative structure. It distinguishes perceptual modeling, regional quality assignment, and rendering-system integration as separate design obligations rather than treating foveated rendering as a single algorithmic trick.

Judder Detection Thresholds

Evidence Excerpt

The isolated-judder experiment found that judder was detectable for predictable and unpredictable motion at 50 Hz and 60 Hz, while observers could not discriminate juddery from non-juddery animations at 72 Hz and 83 Hz. The fitted judder model had RMSE 0.1074 JND.

Page references: Section 6.2.4, Section 6.2.5

Type: statistical_result

This result bounds when judder matters in the model. It supports the claim that the perceptual cost of low refresh rates is not uniform across the 50-165 Hz range and that above the low-70 Hz band blur and resolution effects can dominate.

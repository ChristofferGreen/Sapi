Repeated Resampling Blur

Evidence Excerpt

For constant panning motion, the survey rewrites reprojection as discrete convolution f_{t->t+1} = f_t * k_v with k_v = [v, 1-v]. Repeating the operation convolves the kernel with itself; interpreting k_v as a Bernoulli distribution gives accumulated variance sigma_n^2 = n v(1-v). The text states that bilinear resampling attenuates and misplaces high frequencies.

Page references: pp.2386-2387, Eq.7, Eq.8, Eq.9, Eq.10, Fig.9

Type: equation

This derivation explains a fundamental quality limit of temporal reuse. Even if the cached value is semantically valid, repeated resampling can degrade signal content. The evidence supports periodic recomputation, improved filters, or temporal fade-out as necessary safeguards.

Dynamic Scene Timing Results

Evidence Excerpt

Figure 1 reports a Zero Day frame with 11,000 dynamic emissive triangles rendered in 15 ms and an Amusement Park view with 3.4 million dynamic emissive triangles rendered in 50 ms, both at 1920 x 1080 resolution.

Page references: Fig. 1

Type: figure_result

These timing and scene-size measurements anchor the paper's main practical claim: ReSTIR is not only a theoretical sampling method but a GPU implementation capable of real-time direct lighting in scenes with thousands to millions of dynamic emitters. The evidence is scene-specific and does not by itself establish performance for all hardware or content.

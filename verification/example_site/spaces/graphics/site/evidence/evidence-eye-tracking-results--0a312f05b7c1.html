Eye Pursuit Measurements

Evidence Excerpt

In the eye-tracking experiment, SPEM was not affected by refresh rates above 27.5 Hz but differed by motion type: unpredictable motion produced 4.89 saccades per second versus 1.56 for predictable motion, and tracking delay was 0.092 s versus 0.004 s.

Page references: Section 6.1.5

Type: measurement

This measurement justifies treating predictability as a model input rather than as a narrative qualifier. It shows that unpredictable motion materially changes retinal blur and therefore changes the correct refresh-rate/resolution choice.

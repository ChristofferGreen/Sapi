Forward Reprojection Constraints

Evidence Excerpt

Forward reprojection processes the cache and maps every cached pixel to its new position, avoiding processing of scene geometry for the new frame. The same section notes that this requires a forward motion vector, involves scattering that does not map well to conventional graphics pipelines, can be slow, and may leave holes that require filtering or support-size expansion.

Page references: p.2382, Sec.3.2

Type: formal_argument

This evidence explains both the appeal and the limits of forward reprojection. It can decouple reuse from current-frame geometry processing, which matters for some workloads, but it shifts the problem toward motion-vector quality, visibility resolution, parallel scattering, and hole management.

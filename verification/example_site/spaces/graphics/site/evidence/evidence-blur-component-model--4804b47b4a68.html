Blur Component Formulas

Evidence Excerpt

Hold-type display blur is modeled as bD = v/f, eye-motion blur as bE = pa v + pb, and resolution blur as bR = 1/R. These are converted into Gaussian standard deviations and combined into motion-parallel and orthogonal blur terms.

Page references: Eq.4, Eq.5, Eq.6, Eq.10, Eq.11

Type: equation

These equations make the model interpretable: speed and refresh rate control display blur, eye pursuit contributes a predictability-sensitive term, and image resolution controls spatial blur. The formulas explain why the optimal resolution-refresh trade-off changes with object motion.

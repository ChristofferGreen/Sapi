Experience Alerting Correlation

Evidence Excerpt

Since only the alerting component significantly changed at Time 2 for the retreat group, the authors examined whether months of meditation experience correlated with the magnitude of the alerting score at Time 2. The correlation value was R = -.52 and significant (t = 2.4, p = .03), showing reduced alerting score with increased meditation experience.

Page references: p.115

Type: statistical_result

The correlation links individual differences in prior meditation experience to the retreat group's alerting outcome. It strengthens the interpretation that the Time 2 alerting pattern is related to meditation history, not merely group membership. Its scope is limited because the analysis is correlational within the retreat group and does not establish a randomized causal dose-response effect.

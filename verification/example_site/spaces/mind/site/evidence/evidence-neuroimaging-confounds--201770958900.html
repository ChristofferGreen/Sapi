Neuroimaging Confound Limits

Evidence Excerpt

The source identifies neuroimaging limits including group-average inference, pooling diverse practitioners, cross-sectional designs that preclude cause-and-effect conclusions, head movement, cardiorespiratory effects, vein-drain artifacts, and difficulty estimating practical significance of observed structural or functional brain changes.

Page references: pp. 50-51

Type: formal_argument

This evidence item grounds the paper’s warning that meditation brain images are not self-interpreting proof of clinical benefit or causal neural change. The source acknowledges modest meta-analytic findings while arguing that artifacts and design limits must be handled before practical conclusions are imported into treatment or public messaging.

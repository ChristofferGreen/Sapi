Thalamocortical Optimization Result

Evidence Excerpt

A network whose connection matrix was obtained by optimization for Φ (Φ = 74 bits) has a heterogeneous arrangement of incoming and outgoing connections: each element is connected to a different subset of elements, with different weights. The same amount of connectivity, distributed homogeneously to eliminate functional specialization, yields a complex with much lower values of Φ (Φ = 20 bits). The same amount of connectivity, distributed in such a way as to form four independent modules to eliminate functional integration, yields four separate complexes with much lower values of Φ (Φ = 20 bits).

The simulation result links the biological thalamocortical discussion to the theory’s formal criterion. High Φ appears when a network is both specialized and integrated; removing either property lowers Φ even when the amount of connectivity is held constant. This supports the claim that thalamocortical architecture is a plausible substrate for consciousness.

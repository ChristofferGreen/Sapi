Minimum Bipartition Defines Phi

Evidence Excerpt

The minimum information bipartition MIB(S), the bipartition for which the normalized effective information reaches a minimum, and the corresponding value of Φ(S). To identify complexes and obtain their capacity for information integration, one considers every subset S of X composed of k elements, with k = 2,..., n. For each subset S, we consider all bipartitions and calculate EI(A ↔ B) for each of them.

Page references: pp.4-5

Type: formal_argument

This procedure converts the theory into an auditable measurement recipe. Instead of asking only how much information a system can represent, it asks how much causally effective information survives the weakest partition. The weakest-link construction is why Φ is meant to capture unified integration rather than total entropy or connectivity count.

Alpha Predicts Attentional Lapses

Evidence Excerpt

In the sustained attention task discussed by the review, alpha-band power was much higher before performance errors and could predict errors up to 20 s before they occurred. The same experiment found no differences in SSVEP amplitude between periods preceding erroneous and correct performance.

Page references: p.8

Type: observational_result

This result sharpens the interpretation of alpha suppression. Alpha power can forecast lapses in attention, but the absence of a low-level SSVEP difference suggests that alpha's behavioral effect may operate through attentional control or access rather than simply weakening all early sensory encoding.

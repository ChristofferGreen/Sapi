Threefold S-ART Training Claim

Evidence Excerpt

Within this framework, mindfulness is described to reduce such biases through specific forms of mental training that develop meta-awareness of self (self-awareness), an ability to effectively manage or alter one’s responses and impulses (self-regulation), and the development of a positive relationship between self and other that transcends self-focused needs and increases prosocial characteristics (self-transcendence).

Type: formal_argument

This passage is the core formal argument for the review's S-ART framework. It defines mindfulness not by a single attentional operation but by three functional capacities that jointly transform self-processing. The excerpt matters because later neurobiological and clinical claims are organized around these three capacities rather than around a generic mindfulness label.

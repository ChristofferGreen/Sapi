Qualia Space Matrix

Evidence Excerpt

The values of effective information among the elements of a complex, by defining the relationships among these dimensions, specify the structure of this space. The effective information matrix defines the set of informational relationships, or “qualia space” for each complex. The effective information matrix is different for the two complexes, even though Φ is the same.

Page references: Fig.2, pp.7-8

Type: formal_argument

This is the main evidence for the theory’s distinction between quantity and quality. Two systems can have the same amount of integrated information while differing in the pattern of effective-information relations among their elements. The theory therefore treats conscious content as structure in a relational space, not as a scalar level alone.

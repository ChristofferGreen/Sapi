Self-Processing and Integrated Conscious Experience

Structure: wiki

Integrated Experience

Information Integration Theory offers a broader consciousness framework in which subjective experience is tied to integrated informational structure within complexes, with both quantity and quality specified by integrated information and effective-information relationships.

Mindfulness and Self-Processing

S-ART distinguishes experiential enactive, experiential phenomenological, and narrative self-processing systems and proposes that mindfulness can reduce biased narrative self-processing through regulation and decentering.

Synthesis Boundary

The shared concept is not that these sources use the same theory, but that both treat conscious subjectivity as structured by relations among specialized processes: S-ART focuses on self-related networks and practice-related modulation, while IIT focuses on integrated complexes and qualia-space structure.

Claims Used by This Topic

  • Complexes are experiential subjects

    A complex is an integrated subset with Φ greater than zero that is not contained in a larger subset with higher Φ, and complexes are the loci of subjective experience.

  • Thalamocortical networks maximize Phi

    A distributed thalamocortical network is well suited to generate consciousness because it combines functional specialization with functional integration.

  • Narrative bias reifies schemas

    Biased narrative self-processing can reify dysfunctional self-schemas through affect-biased attention, rumination, and repeated maladaptive interpretation.

  • Consciousness integrates information

    Consciousness corresponds to a system’s capacity to integrate information, because conscious experience is both highly differentiated and unified.

  • Current states specify experiences

    Each particular conscious experience is specified by the current activity values of the variables that mediate informational interactions within a complex.

  • Fronto-parietal control integrates self

    Mindfulness-based meditation is proposed to modulate self-specifying and narrative self networks through an integrative fronto-parietal control network.

  • Mindfulness reduces self-bias

    Mindfulness can be modeled as systematic mental training that reduces biased self-processing by developing self-awareness, self-regulation, and self-transcendence.

  • Self-processing has three systems

    S-ART distinguishes experiential enactive, experiential phenomenological, and narrative self-processing systems as targets of mindfulness-related modulation.

  • Qualia space determines quality

    The quality of consciousness is specified by the informational relationships among the elements of a complex, represented as an effective-information-defined qualia space.

The synthesis is useful because it does not collapse S-ART into Information Integration Theory. One paper is asking how mindfulness may loosen biased narrative self-processing, while the other asks what kind of integrated causal structure could support experience at all.

That boundary matters. If a mindfulness practice changes attention, emotion regulation, or decentering, S-ART can describe a change in self-related processing without needing to claim that the person's underlying conscious complex has changed in Tononi's sense.

Lina Kovac

I kept noticing that both accounts push against treating consciousness as a list of isolated contents. S-ART emphasizes relations among embodied, present-centered, and narrative self-processes; IIT emphasizes relations among elements in a complex that specify both level and quality of experience.

Insightful24 points

I like reading the topic as a warning against two simplifications at once: mindfulness is not just calm attention, and consciousness is not just information processing. In both cases, the structure of relations among processes is doing much of the explanatory work.

The most interesting tension is measurement. S-ART points toward longitudinal work on practice quality, dose, and adverse effects, while IIT has the formal elegance of Φ but faces the problem of applying it to real thalamocortical dynamics.

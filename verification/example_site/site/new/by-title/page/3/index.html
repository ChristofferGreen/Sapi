  • Temporal and Perceptual Quality Allocation in Real-Time RenderingMay 05, 2026TopicEvidence: 0Claims: 9Authors: Daniel Scherzer, Lei Yang, Oliver Mattausch, ...

  • Tradeoff-aware compiler strategies and empirical validationApr 26, 2026TopicEvidence: 0Claims: 14Authors: Pohua P. Chang, Scott A. Mahlke, Wen-mei W. Hwu, ...

    The sources frame compiler optimization as a tradeoff problem whose success must be measured rather than assumed.   • Visual control tradeoffs in computed graphicsApr 26, 2026TopicEvidence: 0Claims: 7Authors: Ozan Ersoy, Christophe Hurter, Fernando V Paulovich, ...
